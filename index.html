About me

I’m a Melbourne-based virtual assistant with a varied background. From nursing and case management to working as a business analyst and software developer. I’m also an artist in my spare time.

A lot of my work has involved learning new systems, understanding how people work, organising information, solving problems and turning mess into something easier to manage.

I’m neurodivergent myself, so I understand the need for clarity and flexible support.

If there's something taking up your time or sitting on your to-do list, let me know and we’ll see how I can help.
